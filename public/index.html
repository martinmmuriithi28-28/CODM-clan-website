          Who can do what

          1. Everyone: sees the roster, scrims and updates with no account. New people tap “Add me to the roster” and wait in Pending for a leader to approve.
          2. Leaders and Vice Masters: log in with their personal code. They approve joins, edit and remove members, post updates and make scrims.
          3. Master: can also give the Leader, Vice Master or Master role, and make codes for leaders.
          4. Login codes: use “Login codes” below to make a code for a leader or any member, then send it on WhatsApp.

          WhatsApp numbers and reminders

          1. Numbers are visible to leaders and to the member who entered it. Other members see only names.
          2. When you tag someone or put them in a scrim team, tap “WhatsApp reminders”. Each person has a button that opens a ready message in WhatsApp.
          3. WhatsApp sends the message only when you tap Send. Automatic sending needs the WhatsApp Business service and a server, which is a later upgrade.
